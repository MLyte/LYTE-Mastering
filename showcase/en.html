PHASELIMITER ENGINE Always on your computer.

Auto — Hard Techno

Density.
Weight. Impact.

Auto mode offers three Hard Techno profiles — Fidèle, Dense and Agressif — to compare with the unmastered source. Compare integrated loudness, true peak, low end and AAC compatibility. If no profile reaches the target but a WAV meets the other limits, Auto suggests a target based on its measured level and lets you render again; the new measurements determine whether it is met.

  1. 01

    Compare three profiles

    PhaseLimiter processes the mix with three predefined profiles. Each combines a different intensity, low-end priority and saturation settings.

    Outputs are measured in integrated LUFS. Final calibration seeks the shared target while maintaining a −1 dBTP WAV ceiling.

    The target can remain out of reach: the app then shows the achieved level and its difference from the target.

  2. 02

    Read the measured changes

    The recommendation considers crest-factor loss and low-end change. Limits depend on the profile: they are stricter for Fidèle and wider for Agressif.

    A local AAC encode/decode check flags peaks above 0 dBTP. This warning is separate from WAV selection: it does not automatically make the render ineligible.

    These measurements cannot fully judge kick attack, pumping or kick / rumble separation. Listen for these before exporting.

  3. 03

    Choose while listening

    All three renders are loaded automatically into the comparator. Their analyses stay visible during playback: LUFS, true peak, dynamics and low-end changes, AAC checks and diagnostics.

    The recommendation guides your choice without certifying sound quality. If no profile stays within its limits, no recommendation is shown; all three renders remain available to listen to and export.

    A separate comparator also lets you browse a local folder and compare up to four WAV, FLAC, MP3 or M4A files, at matched or original levels.

01

Target & intensity

In Manual mode, the dB target is an instruction to the engine, not a promise of output LUFS. Measured output loudness also depends on your mix and the processing.

Intensity controls the amount of processing. Adjust it independently of the target to find your balance between density and dynamics.

REFERENCE · MATCHING LEVEL
02

Preserving the low end

In Manual mode, enable Preserve bass when the low end needs priority. This option changes the engine’s weighting.

Compare the output with and without the option: kick weight, sub sustain and rumble definition. The option’s name does not guarantee an unchanged low end.

PRESERVE BASS · LOW-END FOCUS
03

Auto — Hard Techno

Use the built-in Hard Techno reference or import your own. Its measured level caps the Auto target, within −9 to −2 LUFS; you can request a quieter level.

Reference similarity compares measured characteristics of the analysed passages. It is not a sound-quality score.

After choosing an Auto render, you can also export a 256 kb/s AAC copy in M4A format. The app measures its peaks after decoding and attenuates that copy if needed; the WAV remains unchanged.
